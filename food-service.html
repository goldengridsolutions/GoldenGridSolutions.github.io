The Problem

Energy is one of your largest
controllable costs.

In food service, refrigeration, HVAC, and kitchen loads run from open to close — and often through the night. Most operators treat this as fixed overhead. It isn't.

❄️
Refrigeration runs around the clock
Walk-ins, prep fridges, and display units cycle their compressors continuously — even overnight. Compressors work far harder than they need to, wasting energy every hour of every day at every location in your estate.

Electrical waste you can't see
Kitchen motors, exhaust fans, HVAC units, and compressors create electrical noise that wastes energy across every circuit in your restaurant — invisibly, continuously, at scale.

The savings potential is material at any scale
Whether you operate one location or thousands, the same verified technology applies. For multi-site operators, consistent per-location savings compound into a significant portfolio-wide outcome.

Where it sits

Connected at your panel. Felt across the restaurant.

FilterPro connects in parallel at your main electrical panel. It carries no load and conditions power quality at the source, so your refrigeration, HVAC, exhaust fans and kitchen equipment all draw cleaner power.

Refrigeration & Cooling installs at the individual unit — sensor-driven control at the evaporator trims compressor runtime on specific walk-in, freezer and cooling loads for a deeper reduction.

Your equipment sees cleaner power — and your refrigerant, setpoints, and controls stay exactly as they are.

Why it works

Proprietary technology. Real reductions.

Motors and compressors draw more power than the work they do actually requires. The difference is lost to electrical noise and reactive losses before it ever reaches the equipment — it never shows as a fault, and it appears only on the bill.

FilterPro improves the quality of the supply reaching those loads. Refrigeration & Cooling works on a different mechanism entirely — it detects when a unit has hit its setpoint and pauses the compressor at peak efficiency rather than letting it run on.
