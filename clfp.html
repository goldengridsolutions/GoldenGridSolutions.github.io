Global food distribution center · large refrigerated warehouse
"With refrigeration representing the majority of total electrical usage, even small efficiency gains make a material financial impact — and the results far exceeded expectations."

A major food distribution center operated five chilled cold rooms and three high-load freezer systems. Each freezer ran at 68.5 kW continuously. The pilot deployed an AI-driven optimization layer that reduces compressor runtime by detecting when optimal cooling is reached — while holding setpoint temperatures exactly.

Across 34 days of winter measurement, the system delivered 26.5% refrigeration energy reduction and 219+ kWh saved per day across the freezer rooms alone. Because California refrigeration loads typically rise 25% in warmer months, the full-year figure was adjusted upward to 112,432 kWh annually, with an 11-month payback.

Why this matters for California food producers: the case study site was an industrial cold storage operation, not a CLFP member specifically. The technology and the measured savings are not refrigerant-specific — they reduce compressor runtime regardless of whether the system uses NH3, CO2, or HFC.
