Why it matters

Capital equipment. Not another operating line item.

Refrigeration is 40–60% of a food processing plant's electric bill. Every point off refrigeration compresses directly into gross margin — and the savings survive whether CAISO rates swing up or down.

Capital, not operating expense
You buy the device. You depreciate it. Your accounting team classifies it correctly the first time. No SaaS fee that eats into the savings. No ongoing consulting relationship required.
The savings survive rate volatility
Utility rates are the least controllable line item on your P&L. Cutting the underlying kWh means the savings are locked in whether CAISO markets swing up or down.
Zero production risk
No refrigerant change. No setpoint change. No controls modification. Product quality, food safety compliance, and cold-chain integrity are unaffected.
Built for CLFP-member operations

Whether you run canneries, olive presses, dairy plants, dehydrators, or freezer lines — the underlying problem is the same.

Compressors running longer than they need to. Heat exchange working harder than it has to. Electrical losses upstream of the compressor. The technology addresses all three regardless of refrigerant.
