Where it sits

Between your power and your equipment.

FilterPro sits inline at your main electrical panel. Utility power flows through it before reaching your refrigeration, chillers, motors, and pumps — delivering cleaner power to everything downstream.

Refrigeration & Cooling sits further downstream, inline on the electrical circuits feeding individual HVAC, chiller, and refrigeration units — delivering a deeper reduction on those specific loads.

Your equipment sees cleaner power — and your refrigerant, setpoints, and controls stay exactly as they are.

Why it works

Proprietary technology. Real reductions.

enPact improves power quality at the source using proprietary carbon nanotube (CNT) and spintronics-based dielectric materials. The technology reduces electrical noise and reactive losses across inductive loads — motors, compressors, and refrigeration equipment.

Result: measurable kWh reduction, demonstrated against real operating conditions and validated via IPMVP-aligned M&V.

Why it matters

Capital equipment. Not another operating line item.

Refrigeration is 40–60% of a food processing plant's electric bill. Every point off refrigeration compresses directly into gross margin — and the savings survive whether CAISO rates swing up or down.

Unlike controls upgrades, refrigerant changes, or major mechanical retrofits, enPact installs in parallel during normal operation. Performance measurement begins immediately after installation, with reductions formally validated through M&V.
