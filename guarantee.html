The Emissis Savings Guarantee

Not a purchase.
An investment with a
guaranteed payback.

Most efficiency projects ask you to trust a projection. This one puts the payback period in writing — and if verified savings fall short of forecast, Emissis credits back the capital difference to restore the payback you were quoted.

The reframe

Why this changes the capital conversation.

A typical equipment purchase carries the performance risk on your side of the table: you pay, and the return depends on whether the projections hold. The enPact suite is structured differently. Emissis guarantees the payback period set out in your proposal — the figure that follows from the forecast energy savings and the capital cost.

If the verified savings come in below the forecast kWh, the payback would otherwise stretch. In that event, Emissis credits back the difference in capital cost needed to restore the original payback period — so the payback you were quoted is met at the verified rate of saving. The downside risk sits with Emissis, not with your capital budget.

In one sentence
You buy the equipment, you own it, you depreciate it — and the payback period on that capital is guaranteed in writing, verified by independent-methodology M&V, with the workings open to inspection.
The mechanics

How savings are measured and verified.

Emissis measures and verifies savings using IPMVP, an internationally accepted Measurement & Verification methodology, validated for accuracy and bias.

1
Baseline assessment
A pre-installation site survey establishes baseline kWh consumption and equipment performance under normal operating conditions.
2
Forecast savings calculation
Savings projections are modelled from validated historical consumption data, run-time analysis, and equipment efficiency profiles.
3
Post-installation monitoring
Consumption data is collected through site meters, sub-metering, or energy management systems — a like-for-like operational comparison.
4
Savings verification
Actual performance is compared against the baseline and normalised for external factors such as weather and occupancy. Verified results are set out in a formal savings verification report.
5
Capital rebate if needed
If verified savings fall short of the forecast kWh, Emissis credits back the difference in capital cost required to restore the original payback period set out in your proposal.
The fine print, plainly

Conditions of the guarantee.

The guarantee is assessed against the formal savings verification report — not interim monitoring — under the same operational conditions used to model the forecast. It applies provided that:

  • the equipment is operated and maintained in line with manufacturer recommendations, within the same operational parameters recorded at the time of survey;
  • no major change to site operation, occupancy, or load profile occurs during the measurement period that would materially affect consumption;
  • all installed enPact systems remain in active use and unaltered throughout the monitoring period; and
  • the monitoring subscription remains active throughout the assessment period.
Equipment coverage

Separate from the guarantee: the product warranty.

The Savings Guarantee addresses the energy savings outcome. The equipment itself is covered separately, with terms that depend on the product. For the enPact FilterPro and enPact Refrigeration & Cooling devices — the products covered by the Savings Guarantee statement — Emissis warrants against defects in materials and workmanship for five years from original supply, with repair or replacement at Emissis’s expense, replacement units free of charge, and change-outs carried out by the accredited installing partner.

Other Emissis solutions carry their own coverage — enPact Chillers is engineered for a 10+ year performance lifespan with no recurring service requirements, and Pureflow includes a 10-year manufacturer’s limited warranty. The exact warranty terms that apply to your equipment are set out in your proposal.

The device warranty applies to units installed by accredited partners and commissioned onto the Emissis monitoring platform, with the monitoring subscription kept active. A unit performing to specification is warranted under the Product Warranty; the payback period it contributes to is guaranteed under the Emissis Savings Guarantee.
